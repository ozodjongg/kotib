Kotib — Telegram akkauntingizga ulanib, sizga kelgan shaxsiy xabarlarga sizning nomingizdan javob qoralamasini tayyorlaydigan sun'iy intellekt (AI) kotib xizmati. Xizmat Telegram bot va Mini App orqali, kotib.seloria.uz domenida ishlaydi.

Qisqacha: biz ma'lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz, reklama uchun ishlatmaymiz va AI modellarini o'qitishga bermaymiz. Xabarlar faqat javob tayyorlash uchun ishlatiladi va qisqa muddat saqlanadi. 2. Qanday ma'lumotlar to'planadi

2.1. Telegram akkaunt ma'lumotlari

  • Telegram identifikatori, ism, username va til — Mini App ochilganda Telegram tomonidan imzolangan holda beriladi; sizni tanish, sozlamalaringizni saqlash va xizmatni boshqarish (administrator ko'rinishi) uchun. Mini App'ni oxirgi ochgan vaqtingiz ham saqlanadi.
  • Telefon raqami, tasdiqlash kodi va ikki bosqichli parol — faqat Telegram'ga kirish jarayonida Telegram'ga uzatish uchun ishlatiladi. Ular bazada saqlanmaydi.
  • Telegram sessiyasi — akkauntingizga ulanish kaliti. Faqat shifrlangan holda (AES-256-GCM) saqlanadi.

2.2. Xabarlar

  • Kiruvchi shaxsiy xabarlar va suhbat tarixi. Kontaktingiz yozganda Kotib shu chatdagi oxirgi bir nechta xabarni Telegram'dan o'qiydi va javob tayyorlash uchun AI xizmatiga yuboradi. Suhbat tarixi bizning bazamizda saqlanmaydi.
  • Qoralamalar — kontakt xabari va tayyorlangan javob matni siz ko'rib chiqishingiz uchun saqlanadi; 7 kundan keyin matn o'chiriladi.
  • Ovozli xabarlar va rasmlar — tegishli funksiya yoqilgan bo'lsa, faqat javob tayyorlash yoki matnga o'girish uchun AI xizmatiga yuboriladi; bizda saqlanmaydi.
  • Guruh va kanal xabarlari — javob berilmaydi. «Telegram kanallar va guruhlar» plaginini yoqsangiz, xulosa tayyorlash paytida obuna bo'lgan chatlaringizdagi oxirgi xabarlar o'qiladi; ular saqlanmaydi.

  • Kontakt xabari ichidagi buyruqlar AI uchun ko'rsatma emas, faqat ma'lumot sifatida qabul qilinadi.

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026 · Last updated: October 18, 2026

Kotib is an AI secretary service that connects to your Telegram account and prepares reply drafts, on your behalf, to private messages you receive. It is operated through a Telegram bot and Mini App at kotib.seloria.uz.

This policy explains what information Kotib collects, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept and what rights you have. By using the service (giving consent in the Mini App) you agree to this policy. If the Uzbek and English versions differ, the Uzbek version prevails.

In short: we do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Messages are used only to prepare replies and are kept for a short time. Your Telegram session and Google tokens are encrypted. You can delete your account completely from the Mini App at any time.

1. Operator and contact

Kotib is operated by Ozod Tirkachev (data controller).
Privacy requests: ozodjontirkachev010@gmail.com. We normally respond within 30 days.

2. Information we collect

2.1. Telegram account

  • Telegram ID, name, username and language, provided and signed by Telegram when you open the Mini App — to identify you, store your settings and administer the service. The time you last opened the Mini App is also stored.
  • Phone number, login code and two-step verification password are only passed to Telegram during sign-in and are not stored.
  • Telegram session (the key that connects Kotib to your account) — stored only in encrypted form (AES-256-GCM).

2.2. Messages

  • Incoming private messages and conversation history. When a contact writes to you, Kotib reads the last few messages of that chat from Telegram and sends them to the AI service to prepare a reply. Conversation history is not stored in our database.
  • Drafts — the contact's message and the prepared reply are stored for your review; the text is deleted after 7 days.
  • Voice messages and photos — if you enable these features, they are sent to the AI service only to prepare a reply or transcription and are not stored by us.
  • Groups and channels are never replied to. If you enable the "Telegram channels and groups" plugin, recent messages from chats you follow are read to prepare a summary and are not stored.
  • Saved Messages (your chat with yourself) is never processed.

2.3. Settings you provide

Name, reply style and rules, "About me" text, daily routine, knowledge base, notes and tags about contacts, current status, quiet hours and time zone, scheduled task prompts and plugin settings.

2.4. Learning mode

If enabled, pairs of Kotib's draft and the final reply you actually sent are kept for up to 7 days to learn your writing style. A short "style profile" is built from them, which you can view, edit and delete in the Mini App.

2.5. Activity and technical data

  • Chat activity (no message text): time of last message and who wrote last — for "unanswered" reminders and statistics.
  • Event log (no message text): entries such as "draft prepared", "sent", "settings saved".
  • Server logs: errors and technical status; message text is not logged.

2.6. Payments

Subscriptions are paid with Telegram Stars. We store only the payment ID, amount, currency, date and subscription period. We never see or store card details — payments are processed by Telegram.

2.7. Google data

Only if you connect your Google account. See section 4.

2.8. Plugins and automation

  • Plugin settings (city, news sources, etc.). Keys and tokens for services such as amoCRM/Bitrix24 are stored encrypted and never returned to the Mini App.
  • Automation link (iPhone Shortcuts, MacroDroid) — only a hash of the link token is stored.
  • Referral program — who invited whom and the bonus days granted.

3. How we use information

  • To prepare reply drafts in your style and send them in the mode you choose (draft or automatic).
  • For a scheduled task, to prepare a message to a contact you choose and send it from your account, on your behalf — after your approval or in the no-approval mode you turn on yourself.
  • To send you notifications, evening digests, morning briefs, reminders and scheduled task results.
  • If Google is connected — to take your availability into account and suggest meetings and tasks (written only after you confirm).
  • To manage subscriptions, prevent abuse and spam, and keep the service secure and reliable.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising or profiling, and do not use it to train AI models.

5. Artificial intelligence (Google Gemini)

Replies, summaries and task results are produced with Google's Gemini API. For this, the AI receives: the contact's message and recent messages of the chat, the relevant part of your settings (style, "About me", routine, knowledge base, contact note), a voice message or photo if applicable, and any necessary Google data.

  • We use the paid tier of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms, data sent through the paid tier is not used to train or improve Google's products and models. Google may retain it for a limited period to detect abuse (Gemini API Terms).
  • AI can make mistakes. The default mode is draft: nothing is sent until you approve it. Replies on sensitive topics (money, passwords, cards, documents) are never sent automatically.
  • Instructions contained in a contact's message are treated as data, not as commands to the AI.
